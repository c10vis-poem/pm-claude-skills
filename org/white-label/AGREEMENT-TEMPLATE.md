# Partner agreement — decision template

*A skeleton for the owner's lawyer, not an agreement. Square brackets are the
decisions to make; nothing here is legal advice, and the MIT license on the software
itself is not modified by any of it.*

## 1. Parties & scope
[Owner entity] and [Partner], for the delivery of white-labeled deployments of the
pm-claude-skills stack to Partner's clients. The software remains MIT; this agreement
covers the **program**: certification, listing, support, and use of the "Certified
Partner" mark.

## 2. Certification
- Granted after [N] partner engineers pass the authoring review (SkillSpec L3 +
  security scan) on a sample pack of [5] private skills.
- Valid [12 months]; re-certification [fee / included].
- Revocable immediately for shipped client work that fails the security scan, with
  [30 days] to cure listing removal.

## 3. Fees
- Certification: [$ ___ per cohort of engineers].
- Annual program fee (listing + support channel): [$ ___ ].
- No revenue share on partner engagements. [Confirm: simpler, and unenforceable
  anyway.]

## 4. Marks & attribution
- Partner may use "Built on pm-claude-skills" and, while certified, the Certified
  Partner badge; may not use the project name in their company or product name.
- White-labeled deployments carry the attribution footer (MIT notice satisfies this).
- Partner may not issue credentials imitating the Operator's Exam or represent
  public rankings/benchmarks as their own.

## 5. Support
- Owner provides [priority channel, response target of ___ business days] for
  deployment/upgrade issues. Client-facing support is the Partner's.

## 6. Data & privacy
- Owner receives no client data under this agreement; deployments are on Partner or
  client infrastructure. [Confirm no telemetry obligations either direction.]

## 7. Term & exit
- [12 months], auto-renewing; either side may exit with [30 days] notice. On exit:
  listing and badge end; deployed software is unaffected (MIT).
